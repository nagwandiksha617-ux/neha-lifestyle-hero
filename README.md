# NEHA Lifestyle Hero

Create the NEHA Lifestyle luxury fashion e-commerce website featuring the luxury hero section.

Create a premium, realistic luxury fashion e-commerce hero section inspired by high-end Indian fashion brands.

HERO LAYOUT:
- Full-width desktop hero with a clean, sophisticated composition.
- Use a realistic photograph of Jal Mahal, Jaipur as the background.
- Jal Mahal should be softly visible and slightly blurred, NOT dark, dramatic, fantasy-like, or heavily edited.
- Add a very subtle warm ivory/blush overlay so the background feels elegant and supports the text.
- Keep the overall palette: Soft Blush Pink, Dusty Rose Pink, White, Dark Charcoal and Soft Gold.

PRODUCT:
- Use the exact uploaded featured ornate golden clutch product image as the hero product.
- Remove/clean its original background professionally.
- Keep the product realistic and preserve its actual shape, details, embroidery, stones, pearls and metallic finish.
- Do NOT redesign or invent the product.
- Do NOT make it look like an AI-generated product.
- Place the clutch prominently on the RIGHT side.
- Give it a very subtle natural studio shadow.
- The product should look like a premium e-commerce product photograph.

BRAND:
Use the uploaded official NEHA Lifestyle logo.
Do not recreate or modify the logo.

TEXT ON LEFT:
Small eyebrow:
NEHA LIFESTYLE

Main headline:
Where Elegance Meets Everyday Luxury

Supporting text:
Discover Bags & Jewellery Made to Make You Shine

CTA buttons:
SHOP BAGS
EXPLORE JEWELLERY

DESIGN STYLE:
- Minimal
- Elegant
- Premium
- Feminine
- Modern Indian luxury
- Realistic photography
- Spacious layout
- Sophisticated typography
- Soft gold accents
- Clean white/ivory text area where needed

IMPORTANT:
Do NOT use a model.
Do NOT create a collage.
Do NOT add random jewellery or extra products.
Do NOT use dark red/black cinematic backgrounds.
Do NOT make the hero look like an AI poster.
Do NOT add excessive decorative elements.
Do NOT distort the uploaded product or logo.

The final result should look like a real premium Indian fashion e-commerce homepage, not an AI-generated promotional poster.

Make the hero fully responsive for desktop, tablet and mobile.
