# NEHA Lifestyle Luxury Homepage

## Build
- Create a full-width, responsive homepage hero with a softly blurred Jal Mahal background and warm ivory/blush treatment.
- Place the supplied NEHA Lifestyle logo in a restrained top navigation without redrawing it.
- Professionally isolate the supplied ornate golden clutch, preserving its shape, stones, pearls, chains, and metallic detail.
- Compose the supplied headline, supporting copy, and two shopping actions on the left, with the clutch prominent on the right.
- Adapt the composition for desktop, tablet, and mobile while keeping the product and text clear.

## Visual direction
- Use soft blush, dusty rose, ivory, charcoal, and subtle gold accents.
- Favor spacious editorial typography, realistic photography, minimal decoration, and restrained motion.
- Do not add models, jewellery, extra products, collages, or cinematic styling.

## Technical details
- Store the supplied images through the project asset system and use an edited transparent product asset.
- Add page-specific social metadata and accessible image/button labels.
- Verify the final homepage visually at desktop and mobile sizes and confirm the preview builds successfully.
